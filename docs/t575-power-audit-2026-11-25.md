# SM-T575 LTE power audit — 2026-11-25

Live Nura/Duranium Phosh, kernel 7.3.0, balanced-battery. Source synchronization is separate from the live system. No flashing or permanent power changes in these tests.

## Audio and cpusets

Disposable sibling slices verified 0-3 confinement for a background service while the independent audio probe and actual headphone service kept 0-7. A subsequent temporary move of the real headphone controller to t575audio.slice failed to discover the PipeWire headphone sink within its existing timeout. Rollback completed and restoring the original system.slice placement recovered audible headphones. Causality not established; no actual system.slice restriction was applied. Keep original runtime controller intact. Separate slice remains a future option. The staged TuneD helper now uses an opt-in explicit background-service allowlist; not yet packaged or live-tested. TAS2562 activation remained N.

## Idle

With LibreWolf and settings closed, Phoc fell from 24 to 0.10 scheduler ticks/s in short samples. CPU idle nearly continuous, WFI only, deeper sleep disabled for all eight cores. Screen-on battery readings 2.72 and 2.75 W (instantaneous). Backlight 84/3977. Panfrost runtime suspended; accumulated runtime stats approximately 96.8% suspended, G3D genpd reported on. GPU physical power-off not established. No MIF/INT devfreq exposed. Boot protections clk_ignore_unused, pd_ignore_unused, regulator_ignore_unused and exynos9810_c2=0 retained.

## Schedutil A/B/A (screen-on quiet, discharging)

10 samples per phase, 3 s apart, 5 s settling; same running headphone controller and no CPU affinity changes.

| Phase | rate_limit_us | Mean W | Mean mA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 150 | 2.159 | 562.9 |
| B | 5000 | 2.263 | 590.6 |
| A2 | 150 | 2.166 | 565.2 |

5000 us was about 4.65% higher than the mean of the two baseline phases. Original 150 us restored; audio process unchanged and speaker locked. This short single-device experiment is not a general power-saver workload result. Frequency ceilings were not changed. Keep 150 us in the current live balanced profile; do not promote 5000 us as a global LTE default. The imported power-saver profile remains source-only and its combined settings require separate validation. Prioritize idle power-domain, memory bus and C2 investigations separately from upstream synchronization.
